APDC 2026 TIMING UPDATE

ALL COMPETITION ROUNDS: 1 dance = 1:20 timetable allocation.
Quarter Final = 1:20 per dance
Semi Final = 1:20 per dance
Final / Grand Final = 1:20 per dance
MC transition time is included in this allocation.

IMPORTANT FIX:
An older Firebase timetable previously overrode the newly uploaded timetable-data.json,
which is why the public SEARCH page still showed 1:00 after uploading the first timing ZIP.
This version prevents an older Firebase timingVersion from masking the deployed 1:20 schedule.
In JUDGE Admin, the packaged 1:20 schedule is loaded when Firebase is older. Press SAVE once
to publish the same schedule to timetableOverride and apdcPublic/timetable for SEARCH / MC / LIVE.
